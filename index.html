02 — About

I hold a PhD in Computational Chemistry (UPM, 2014) with over 15 years of international research experience in quantum simulation, heterogeneous catalysis, and photovoltaic solar energy. Author of 17 scientific publications in RSC, ACS, AIP, and Elsevier journals, and co-inventor of a registered patent at UPM.

Currently in active transition towards Data Science and AI Engineering, combining a solid scientific foundation with Python, PyTorch, scikit-learn, and Graph Neural Networks. Enrolled in an intensive AI & ML Bootcamp (~1,250 h) at SomosF5/AI4Inclusion, working on portfolio projects in materials science and energy.

Certified Scrum Master & Product Owner with 15 years facilitating multidisciplinary teams across Spain, Brazil, and the UK.

Based in Madrid, Spain. Multilingual: Spanish (native), English (C1), Portuguese (advanced), French (intermediate).
